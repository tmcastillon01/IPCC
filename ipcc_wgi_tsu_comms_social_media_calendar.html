This proposed editorial board bridges internal AR7 assessment cycle milestones (Lead Author Meetings, Second Order Draft review windows, and plenary timelines) with recurring UN physical science observances across the year.

Two Complete Sample Posts Included To illustrate tone, calibrated uncertainty phrasing, and visual packaging without clutter, two dates contain full sample copy and asset specs while all other dates hold clean editable placeholders:
Timing Note · AR7 As of 20 November 2026 the Panel has not agreed an AR7 work plan. Its 65th session (Addis Ababa, 12–16 October 2026) ended without a decision on the Working Group timeline. Dates for 2027–2028 review stages, Lead Author Meetings and approval plenaries are therefore proposed and marked TBC. UN observance dates are fixed.
Inter-Agency Coordination Note In practice, all scheduled posts, messaging, and joint campaigns would be coordinated closely with the IPCC Secretariat, Working Groups II & III, and co-parent bodies (WMO and UNEP) to ensure unified terminology and strictly policy-neutral framing (“policy-relevant, never policy-prescriptive”).

Process note

How I built this

  • Each entry pairs a channel with a job (reach, explain, invite questions), so the calendar reads as a plan and not just a list of posts.
  • Anything tied to AR7 dates comes from public IPCC pages. Dates that are only proposed are labelled as proposed.
  • Science content stays inside Working Group I scope. Impacts and mitigation topics are left to WGII and WGIII, or shown as pointers.
  • Any entry that carries a number would be checked by the relevant author before it was scheduled.
