Process note

How I built this

  • The numbers come from the UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026. I kept UNEP’s own wording on overshoot, and on what is projected versus what is only pledged.
  • This is UNEP material, not Working Group I. Emissions pathways sit closer to mitigation than to physical science, so the deck labels its source and does not present these as WGI findings.
  • Projections appear as scenarios or ranges. I avoid wording that turns them into forecasts.
  • Colours are chosen to stay readable for colour-blind viewers and on a projector. There are no red-green pairs, and meaning never depends on colour alone.
  • Neither UNEP nor the IPCC has reviewed this. For a real launch I would ask the report authors to check every number on every slide.
