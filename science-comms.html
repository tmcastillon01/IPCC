Process note

How these samples were built

  • I run the same four checks on every piece: trace the number to its source, keep the report’s confidence language, check it sits inside Working Group I scope, and label clearly what is mine.
  • Sources sit on the page, not in my notes. Anyone reviewing a build can follow a figure back to the chapter or summary it came from.
  • The ice explainer cites WGI for the physical finding and points to Working Group II for what it means for people on the ground, so the boundary stays visible to readers.
  • None of this has been through expert review. The routine I would follow in the TSU is: draft, send each claim and number to the relevant author, revise, then sign-off before anything is published.
  • Every page carries a sample label and a disclaimer, so none of it can be mistaken for official IPCC material.
